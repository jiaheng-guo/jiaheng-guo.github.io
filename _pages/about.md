I am a second-year M.S. student in the [Department of Statistics and Operations Research](https://stor.unc.edu/) at the [University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill](https://www.unc.edu/), where I am fortunate to work with Professor [Tianlong Chen](https://tianlong-chen.github.io/) at UNC CS. I received my B.Eng. in [Artificial Intelligence](https://ai.nju.edu.cn/) from [Nanjing University](https://www.nju.edu.cn/en/) in 2025.

Most of my work sits in **AI for science**, mostly in biology and healthcare. I am specifically interested in:

- **Physiological world models** — learning to simulate living systems under intervention.
- **AI for cancer research** — molecular perturbation models, drug-response prediction, target identification, and drug repurposing.
- **Neural stimulation and response** — modeling how neural systems respond to electrical stimulation.
- **Agentic AI for critical infrastructure** — agent systems that simulate and recommend actions in power distribution networks.

If you want to talk about any of these, or about something adjacent, feel free to email me.
